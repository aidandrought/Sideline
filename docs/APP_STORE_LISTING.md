## Description
Sideline is a football companion app built for fans who want everything around the match in one place.

Track live matches, preview upcoming fixtures, catch up on results, follow your favorite clubs and leagues, and stay close to the conversation through fan communities and live chat.

With Sideline, you can:

- Follow live scores and match updates
- Join club, league, and tournament communities
- Read football news and match-related stories
- Preview fixtures before kickoff
- Check recent results and match details
- Customize the teams and leagues you care about most
- Get match notification reminders and live event alerts

Sideline is designed to make matchday faster and more social, whether you are following your club every week or keeping up with the biggest competitions in world football.
