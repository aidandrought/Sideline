# Sideline Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

Sideline is a football community app that helps you follow teams, leagues, and live match chats. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect
- Account data: email, username, and basic profile information you provide.
- Preferences: followed teams/leagues, notification preferences, and theme settings.
- Usage data: community follows, chat messages, and app interactions needed to deliver features.
- Presence data: live chat presence to show viewer counts.
- Device data: platform type and push notification tokens (only to deliver notifications you opt into).

## How We Use Data
- Provide core functionality: communities, chats, match updates, and live score notifications.
- Personalize your experience and improve performance and reliability.
- Maintain security and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal data.

## Sharing
We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app (e.g., Firebase for authentication, database, and notifications). These providers are required to protect your data.

## Your Choices
You can update your profile, change notification preferences, and manage followed communities. You can request account deletion.

## Data Retention
We retain data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated data.

## Security
We use industry-standard security practices, including authentication controls and database rules, to protect your data.

## Children
Sideline is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Contact
Questions or requests? Contact us at support@sideline.app.
